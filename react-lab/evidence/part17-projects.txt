=== HydrateFallback investigation (react-router 8.4.0) ===
A. HydrateFallback declared only inside the lazy module, app starts on /reports  -> warning IS logged
B. HydrateFallback on the non-lazy parent route, app starts on /reports        -> NO warning
C. app starts on a non-lazy route                                              -> NO warning
conclusion: put HydrateFallback on a NON-lazy route (a lazy module's fallback is not known when the first render happens)
